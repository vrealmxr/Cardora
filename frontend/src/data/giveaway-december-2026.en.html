Cardora Giveaway · November–December 2026

Giveaway terms

The prize: Pokémon TCG 30th Celebration Greninja ex Tin

One winner receives one tin worth €40.00. Entries are welcome worldwide. Cardora covers shipping and any customs duties, import taxes and customs clearance fees, so the winner receives the prize at no cost.

When can I enter?

Entries open on 17 November 2026 at 00:00 and close on 1 December 2026 at 23:59:59. The draw will take place on 2 December 2026. All times are Greece local time.

How do I enter?

Entry is free, with no purchase required, and is open to people aged 18 or over. To receive one basic entry:

  1. Follow the official @cardora.gr account on Instagram.
  2. Register with Cardora. If you already have an account, you do not need to create another one.

No identity document, payment card or Stripe Connect account is required for the basic entry or additional entries from comments. Seller verification is required only if you choose to publish listings. If you win, you must complete identity verification before receiving the prize, as explained below.

How can I get additional entries?

Following @cardora.gr and registering with Cardora gives you one basic entry. You can optionally earn additional entries in either or both of the following ways during the giveaway period:

  • Instagram comments — up to 5 additional entries. Comment on the official giveaway post on @cardora.gr, mentioning one friend by their @username. For each additional entry, leave a separate comment mentioning a different friend. One comment per friend counts, up to five in total. Comments containing more than one tag do not count.
  • Cardora listings — up to 5 additional entries. Each different listing you publish with a photo of the item, a price and a description gives you one additional entry. Incomplete or duplicate listings do not count. To qualify, a listing must remain published and publicly accessible until the giveaway closes on 1 December 2026 at 23:59:59 Greece local time. If it is deleted, withdrawn or removed earlier, that additional entry will not count. Publishing a listing requires all of Cardora's usual seller verification and payment setup requirements. This applies only to optional entries from listings; it is not a requirement for the basic entry or comments.

The two limits are separate. You can receive up to 11 entries in total: 1 basic entry + 5 from comments + 5 from listings. For example, 2 valid comments and 1 qualifying listing give you 4 entries including your basic entry.

How is the winner selected?

Both basic entry steps must be completed before the closing time. Each person is entitled to one basic entry, regardless of how many accounts they hold. A random draw will take place on 2 December: every valid entry, basic or additional, has an equal chance of being selected.

The draw will be shown publicly on the official @cardora.gr Instagram account. The result will be announced in Stories, showing only the confirmed winner's Instagram username, without identity documents, email or delivery details. The public announcement does not replace the personal email notification.

How will I be notified and receive the prize?

If selected, Cardora will notify you at your account email address. You have 48 hours from the time the notification is sent to reply accepting the prize. Please also check your spam folder.

Before delivery, we will confirm that you meet the entry requirements and ask you to complete identity verification within Cardora if you have not already done so. The 48-hour deadline applies to your reply. You do not need to open a seller payment account to receive the prize.

If you do not reply in time, decline the prize, or your eligibility or identity cannot be confirmed, another winner will be selected using the same procedure. After confirmation, we will arrange the delivery address with you privately. Cardora arranges delivery and pays all shipping costs, any customs duties, import taxes and customs clearance fees, including for destinations outside the European Union. The winner pays nothing to receive the prize. Do not send identity documents or banking credentials through Instagram.

Can I exchange or transfer the prize?

The prize cannot be exchanged for money or another item at the winner's request and cannot be transferred to a third party. It is delivered exclusively to the confirmed winner.

When can an entry be excluded?

Cardora reserves the right to disregard invalid entries and disqualify participants where it establishes a breach of these terms, use of fake or multiple accounts to evade entry limits, automated comments, falsified information or other abusive conduct that compromises the draw. Disqualification must be based on documented circumstances and applied proportionately. You may contact Cardora for clarification or reconsideration.

Can the giveaway be changed or cancelled?

Cardora reserves the right to amend, postpone, temporarily suspend or, where no reasonable alternative exists, cancel the giveaway for a serious and documented reason, such as a substantial technical problem, widespread abusive participation, force majeure or inability to supply the prize. If the specified prize becomes unavailable, Cardora will first seek to replace it with a prize of equal or greater value at no cost to the winner.

Any change and its reason will be announced promptly on this page and the official @cardora.gr account. Changes will be limited to what is necessary, respecting already valid entries, equal treatment and applicable law.

How is my information used?

VRealm I.K.E., as the data controller, uses your Cardora account details, Instagram username and, if you claim additional entries, your public comments on the giveaway post (including the mentions they contain) and qualifying listings to validate entries and conduct the draw. Awarding the prize also requires the winner's identity verification result, contact information and delivery details. Only the confirmed winner's Instagram username is used for the public presentation of the draw and announcement of the result in Stories. Only information needed for delivery and, where required, customs clearance is shared with the carrier.

This processing is necessary to perform these giveaway terms. Entering does not subscribe you to promotional emails. Draw records are retained for as long as needed to check entries, deliver the prize and resolve disputes, or as required by law. Identity document details, email and delivery address will not be published in the winner announcement.
